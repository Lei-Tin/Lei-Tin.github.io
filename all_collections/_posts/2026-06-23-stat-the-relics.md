Another game mod. This one's for Slay the Spire 2.

It puts little numbers under your relics so you can see what they've actually done. Extra gold, cards drawn, that sort of thing.

![Relic tooltips with the extra counters from StatTheRelics]({{ '/assets/images/projects/stat-the-relics.png' | relative_url }})

I like being able to hover over something and go "oh, you did all that?" Or almost nothing. Also useful information, I guess.

The stats stay in your run history too, so they're still there if you go back and look later.

Anyway. More numbers to stare at while playing a card game.
